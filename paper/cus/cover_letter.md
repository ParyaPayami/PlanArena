# Cover letter (draft): Computational Urban Science

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "PlanArena: an exact, learning-assisted land-use optimizer with empirical evaluation", for
publication as an Original Paper in *Computational Urban Science*. [If submitting to the collection: We submit it to the
collection "Human-Centered Urban Digital Twins and Policy Insights".]

Land-use decisions in US cities are made at public hearings, where projected effects are summarised in a few
indicators and residents testify but do not vote. The manuscript presents an open urban digital twin and land-use
optimizer, built on official land-use, plan and zoning data for about five million parcels in ten US cities, that
scores any parcel-level change on social, economic and environmental indicators, estimates how likely it is, finds
the optimal plan, and shows how stakeholder votes depend on the decision rule. An exact mixed-integer formulation of
the engine's score certifies the optimal plan of an area in seconds and measures the trade-offs between pillars on the
exact frontier; machine learning corrects the engine's yields with calibrated intervals and proposes optimal plans at
once. Each component is tested against what was built (held-out and out-of-time tests) or against exhaustive
enumeration, and the paper states the type of evidence behind every claim, including where the models fail and where
optimality can only be bounded.
This fits the journal's focus on computational methods and urban digital twins for decision-making.

The manuscript and Online Resource 1 are anonymised for double-blind review. The code (MIT licence) is available to
reviewers through an anonymised repository link, and an online demonstration of the Studio and game is linked in the
text. The work is original, has not been published, and is not under consideration elsewhere. It uses only public
administrative records and involves no human participants. The use of AI tools is described in the Methods section.
